What Is Sedation Dentistry?

Sedation dentistry uses safe medication to help you relax or sleep during dental treatment. Bear Glasgow Dental offers four levels to patients across Delaware: nitrous oxide (laughing gas), a calming pill, IV sedation, and general anesthesia, for patients ages 3 and up. It's a good fit for dental anxiety, phobia, sensitive gag reflex, low pain tolerance, or anyone who wants a more comfortable visit.

  • Phone: (302) 613-0041

  • Who it's for: Patients with dental anxiety, dental phobia, sensitive gag reflex, low pain tolerance, need for multiple procedures, or traumatic dental history
  • Procedures covered: Tooth extractions, wisdom tooth removal, root canals, dental implants, fillings, cleanings, and more

Sedation dentistry uses safe, well-tested medications to help you relax during your visit. It's not only for people with severe phobia. It's for anyone who wants a more comfortable experience, whether that's a routine cleaning or something more involved.

If you've been avoiding the dentist because of fear, a past bad experience, or simply not knowing what to expect, we can help. We see patients from all over Delaware who haven't been to a dentist in years and treat them the same as everyone else: with patience, without judgment, and without any mention of how long it's been.

Before any sedation, our team reviews your full medical history. A licensed clinician watches over you throughout the procedure. You're never left alone, and we don't start until you feel ready.

Sleep Dentistry

Can I Sleep Through My Dental Work?

Yes, or as close to it as is safely possible. Most patients searching for a "sleep dentist near you" are looking for IV sedation, often called sleep dentistry. You're not technically unconscious, but you're in such a deep, relaxed, sleep-like state that almost no one remembers the appointment afterward. Patients regularly tell us, "It felt like I slept through it."

For patients who need to be fully asleep — children with significant special needs, severe phobia, or specific medical situations — we also offer general anesthesia.

Is This For Me?

If Any of This Sounds Like You, Sedation Dentistry Can Help

Sedation dentistry isn't only for people with severe fear or phobia. If you want a more comfortable, painless visit for any reason, you qualify. We will never make you feel bad about how long it's been since your last appointment.

😰

Dental Anxiety & Phobia

If fear of the dentist keeps you from scheduling or completing dental appointments, sedation removes that barrier. Whether you experience mild nervousness or severe dental phobia, we're the calm dentist for nervous patients, and we take your anxiety seriously.

🤢

Sensitive Gag Reflex

Sedation dentistry for sensitive gag reflex patients is one of our most common requests. A strong gag reflex makes X-rays, impressions, and even cleanings uncomfortable, sedation relaxes this response and makes treatment completely tolerable.

🩺

Low Pain Tolerance

Some patients need more than local anesthesia for a truly painless visit. Sedation works alongside numbing so you feel absolutely nothing, even if you've had procedures in the past that weren't as comfortable as they should have been.

📋

Multiple Procedures at Once

Need fillings, extractions, implants, or a full mouth restoration? As a sedation dentist for multiple procedures, we can complete extensive treatment in a single comfortable visit, saving you time, multiple co-pays, and multiple recovery periods.

🧠

Special Needs Patients

Patients with autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, Alzheimer's, cerebral palsy, or other conditions may find dental chairs and procedures overwhelming. Sedation makes treatment accessible and safe.

😔

Traumatic Past Experiences

A painful or frightening dental experience, or even dental trauma from childhood, can create lasting anxiety and dental phobia. Sedation helps you reclaim your comfort and rebuild a positive, relaxed dental experience going forward.

💊

Numbing Shots Don't Fully Work for You

Some patients don't get fully numb from standard injections alone. This is a real and common issue, not a personal failing. Sedation fills that gap so you truly feel nothing during your procedure.

🧒

Nervous Children

Children who are too young, too anxious, or too uncooperative for routine dental work can safely receive nitrous oxide or, in some cases, stronger sedation to complete necessary treatment.

What to Expect

Your Sedation Dentistry Journey: Step by Step

We walk every patient through a clear, comfortable process from consultation to recovery. No surprises, no fear of the unknown. Just a calm, pain-free dental experience from start to finish.

01

Initial Consultation

We review your complete medical and dental history, current medications, and anxiety level. Together we identify the sedation option best suited to your needs and the procedure you're having done.

02

Pre-Procedure Preparation

You'll receive detailed written instructions tailored to your sedation type, what to eat or avoid, how to prepare transportation, and what to expect on the day of your appointment. For oral and IV sedation, we may prescribe a pre-appointment medication to help you sleep well the night before.

03

Day of Your Appointment

Arrive with a trusted companion if required. Free parking is right outside our door at Peoples Plaza. Our team greets you warmly, answers any last questions, and administers your sedation. We wait until you feel fully comfortable before beginning. You can signal us at any time to pause or stop. Your oxygen and heart rate are monitored continuously throughout.

04

Treatment

With sedation in effect, our dentists work efficiently and comfortably, often completing work that would otherwise require multiple visits. You may feel relaxed, drowsy, or fully unaware depending on your sedation level, but you are safe and monitored throughout.

05

Recovery & Aftercare

For nitrous oxide, recovery takes just minutes. For oral and IV sedation, you'll rest comfortably at our office until you're ready to leave with your designated driver. We provide written aftercare instructions and our team is available by phone if you have any questions during your recovery at home.

Your Safety First

Our Commitment to Safe, Monitored Sedation

Every sedation procedure at Bear Glasgow Dental is performed with rigorous safety standards. Our clinical team is trained in sedation protocols, emergency response, and continuous vital sign monitoring.

  • ✓
    Your Full Health History, Reviewed First

    Before we recommend anything, we review your medications, allergies, and health conditions to make sure your sedation choice is the safest fit for you.

  • ✓
    We Watch Over You the Whole Time

    A small clip on your finger tracks your oxygen and heart rate throughout the procedure. You are never left alone while sedated.

  • ✓
    Prepared for Any Situation

    Our office keeps medications on hand that can wake you up quickly if needed, plus oxygen and an AED. Our team runs regular drills so these tools are always ready.

  • ✓
    No Surprises: Clear Instructions Before and After

    You'll know exactly what to do the night before, the morning of, and after you get home. We send you home with written instructions and a number to call if anything feels off.

  • ✓
    You Can Signal Us at Any Time

    You are in control. If you need a moment, need to stop, or just need reassurance, signal our team and we pause immediately. We never continue treatment if you're not comfortable.
